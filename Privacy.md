# PPL Wingman — Privacy Policy

PPL Wingman does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.
There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party tracking.

Speech transcription runs entirely on-device. The app makes network
requests only to fetch publicly available audio streams, public weather
data (Nav Canada, NOAA, Windy), and a one-time speech-recognition
model download.
